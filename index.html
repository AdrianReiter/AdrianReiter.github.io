C++17 · SENSOR FUSION

Visual-Inertial Odometry

Monocular VIO system built on a 15-state Error-State Extended Kalman Filter. Fuses high-rate IMU propagation with Shi-Tomasi / KLT feature tracks and RANSAC outlier rejection, triangulates lost features with Ceres, and renders the live 6-DOF trajectory in Pangolin. Runs on EuRoC and TUM-VI datasets with a full unit-test suite.
